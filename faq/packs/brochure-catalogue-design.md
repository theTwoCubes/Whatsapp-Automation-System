Pricing, page rules, timelines, deliverables, and print for brochure and
catalogue projects. Base pack policies apply on top.

A: No maximum.

# Page-count rule (printing)

!verbatim
Q: Why do pages come in multiples of 4?
A: For printing with centre pinning we design in sets of 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 pages. If you ask for 7 pages we'd recommend 8 — for example, 8 × ₹2,500 = ₹20,000 + 18% GST = ₹23,600.

Q: What if I want an odd number of pages?
A: We'd suggest rounding up to the next print-friendly count (2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20…) so the print binding works cleanly.

# What you get

Q: What's included per page?
A: Page design, image sourcing from our stock subscription, background removal and retouching, and up to 2 infographics or diagrams per page.

Q: How many design directions do you present first?
A: Two cover-and-back-page options based on the brief. Once the cover theme is approved, the inner pages are designed to match.

Q: Do you design one sample page first?
A: Yes — two cover and back page options, then inner pages after theme approval.

Q: How many revision rounds are included?
A: 3 rounds of revisions per page. Beyond that, revisions are charged based on effort.

Q: What counts as one revision round?
A: Any set of changes you want — text, layout, placement, and so on.

Q: What do revisions beyond that cost?
A: Charged based on the effort required.
