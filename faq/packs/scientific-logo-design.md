# What it is

Q: What is scientific logo design?
A: In Indian Vedic tradition, Mantra (intention), Tantra (way of working), and Yantra (a purposeful symbol) are all important. Scientific logo design treats the logo as your business's visual Yantra - aligning shapes, colours, symbolism, and structure with your birth chart, business category, and other scientific parameters, so the logo supports the growth direction you want.

Q: How is this different from a normal logo?
A: A normal logo asks "does it look good?". Scientific logo design also asks "does the logo support the business's growth direction?". Aesthetics stay, but shapes, colours, symbolism, proportions, and scientific parameters are considered alongside them.

Q: Is this only for businesses with problems?
A: No. If you're already doing well and want expansion, better customers, premium positioning, a larger market, or the next growth level, scientific logo design is relevant too. It's about alignment and growth support, not only correcting problems.

Q: Will only changing the logo grow my business?
A: No. Growth depends on marketing, sales, product or service quality, pricing, team, execution, and management. Scientific logo design gives you a growth-supporting foundation. Marketing, sales, and execution still have to happen.

# Framework fundamentals

Q: How does this influence the subconscious mind?
A: Visual symbols are perceived at a subconscious level, not just consciously. The owner, employees, vendors, and customers see the logo repeatedly. The objective is to use shape, colour, symbolism, and structure to reinforce the desired message at that level.

Q: Why call the logo a "Yantra"?
A: A Yantra is a purposeful symbolic structure. The logo is treated as the business's visual Yantra - shapes, colours, directions, and symbolism aligned to the business's purpose.

Q: Why is shape so important?
A: Shapes carry different characteristics. Squares are associated with stability, upward triangles with movement and action, circles with infinity and space. Shape selection is chosen based on the business's objective.

Q: What is the connection between colour and business growth?
A: Colour is part of the logo's visual and energetic language. Different colours carry different associations. Colour is not picked by favourite alone - business category, birth-chart considerations, shape, and overall visual balance are all considered.

Q: Can I use my favourite colour?
A: You can share your preference. If a colour isn't suitable to the business objective or analysis, we may suggest alternatives. The goal is a colour that fits the business more than a colour you personally like.
