# What it is

Q: What is scientific logo design?
A: In Indian Vedic tradition, Mantra (intention), Tantra (way of working), and Yantra (a purposeful symbol) all matter. Scientific Logo Design treats your logo as the business's visual Yantra - aligning shapes, colours, symbolism, and structure with your birth chart, business category, and other scientific parameters, so the identity supports the growth direction you want.

Q: How is this different from a normal logo?
A: A normal logo asks "does it look good?". Scientific Logo Design also asks "does this identity support where the business is trying to go?". The aesthetics stay, but shape, colour, symbolism, proportion, and scientific parameters are weighed alongside them.

Q: Who does this service?
A: Maulik Dave personally, combining 26 years of branding experience with the scientific framework.

Q: Is this only for businesses having problems?
A: Not at all. If you're already doing well and want expansion, better customers, premium positioning, a bigger market, or the next growth level, it's just as relevant. It's about alignment and growth support, not only fixing problems.

Q: Will changing the logo alone grow my business?
A: No, and we won't claim that. Growth depends on marketing, sales, product or service quality, pricing, team, execution, and management. Scientific Logo Design gives you a growth-supporting foundation - the marketing and execution still have to happen.

Q: My sales aren't growing. Is my logo the reason?
A: I wouldn't put it down to the logo, but it's worth looking at from that angle. Sometimes the owner's vision has grown while the identity still represents the older direction. In Scientific Logo Design we check whether the logo is aligned with sales, customer attraction, communication, and the growth objective. The ₹450 existing-logo analysis is how that gets looked at properly, with Maulik reviewing it personally.

Q: Payments keep getting stuck. Is my logo connected?
A: I can't tell you the logo is the cause. But if payment issues recur, the identity is worth examining from the perspective of money flow, stability, customer relationship, and business structure - which is what the ₹450 existing-logo analysis covers.

Q: Clients delay payments. How does this help?
A: Payment delays aren't only a collection issue. The visual identity can be examined from the angle of payment flow, stability, and customer relationship. The ₹450 analysis is where that actually gets looked at against your business category and existing logo.

Q: My deals break at the last moment. Any connection to the logo?
A: If it keeps happening, the pattern is worth examining from the identity's perspective - communication, customer relationship, stability, direction, and conversion signals. The ₹450 existing-logo analysis is where Maulik looks at that personally.

Q: Customers say yes and then cancel.
A: A recurring pattern like that is worth analysing. A logo communicates subconsciously, so we check whether the visual identity is properly supporting trust, communication, customer connection, and stability.

Q: We get new customers but few repeat customers.
A: Repeat business matters a lot. Existing customers, new customers, and communication are all considered in the framework, so if repeat business is weak the existing logo can be examined from that angle.

Q: We can't get new customers.
A: A logo doesn't bring in customers directly, but visual identity shapes perception and communication. Colour, symbolism, visual attraction, and new-customer orientation are all weighed against the business's growth objective.

Q: I can't find or keep good staff.
A: The team is a major growth factor, and in this framework identity also covers employees and internal relationships. If it's a recurring issue, the logo's structure can be examined from the angles of stability, relationships, and organisational needs - though hiring, training, and management systems matter just as much.

Q: My business is profitable but growth has plateaued. Should I redesign?
A: Possibly worth evaluating. Profitable and growing aren't the same thing, so we'd check whether the existing logo only represents the current business or also supports where you want to go next.

# Framework fundamentals

Q: How does a logo influence the subconscious mind?
A: Visual symbols are taken in at a subconscious level, not only consciously. The owner, employees, vendors, and customers all see the logo repeatedly, so the aim is to use shape, colour, symbolism, and structure to reinforce the intended message at that level.

Q: Why call the logo a Yantra?
A: A Yantra is a purposeful symbolic structure. The logo is treated as the business's visual Yantra, with shapes, colours, directions, and symbolism aligned to its purpose.

Q: Why is shape so important?
A: Different shapes carry different characteristics - squares with stability, upward triangles with movement and action, circles with infinity and space. The shape is chosen from the business's objective.

Q: What is the connection between colour and business growth?
A: Colour is part of the logo's visual and energetic language, and different colours carry different associations. It isn't picked by favourite alone - business category, birth-chart considerations, shape, and overall visual balance all come into it.

Q: Can I use my favourite colour?
A: You can absolutely share your preference. If a colour isn't suitable for the business objective or the analysis, we may suggest alternatives - the goal is a colour that fits the business rather than one that's personally liked.

Q: Do you need my birth chart?
A: Yes, birth-chart analysis is part of the framework, alongside the business category and the other parameters. Maulik takes those details directly once you've started.

Q: What is a Muhurata and why does it matter here?
A: It's a chosen auspicious timing. Selecting a suitable Muhurata is one of the steps considered when the identity is developed and put into use.
