# What it is

Q: What is scientific logo design?
A: In Indian Vedic tradition, Mantra (intention), Tantra (way of working), and Yantra (a purposeful symbol) are all important. Scientific logo design treats the logo as your business's visual Yantra - aligning shapes, colours, symbolism, and structure with your birth chart, business category, and other scientific parameters, so the logo supports the growth direction you want.

Q: How is this different from a normal logo?
A: A normal logo asks "does it look good?". Scientific logo design also asks "does the logo support the business's growth direction?". Aesthetics stay, but shapes, colours, symbolism, proportions, and scientific parameters are considered alongside them.

Q: Is this only for businesses with problems?
A: No. If you're already doing well and want expansion, better customers, premium positioning, a larger market, or the next growth level, scientific logo design is relevant too. It's about alignment and growth support, not only correcting problems.

Q: Will only changing the logo grow my business?
A: No. Growth depends on marketing, sales, product or service quality, pricing, team, execution, and management. Scientific logo design gives you a growth-supporting foundation. Marketing, sales, and execution still have to happen.

# The pain-point conversation (do not diagnose, do not blame the logo)

The bot should never say "your payment is stuck because of your logo" or
"changing the logo will grow your sales." Instead: "this can be analysed from
the logo's perspective too - Maulik can share observations personally in a
15-minute Zoom."

Q: My sales aren't growing. Is my logo the reason?
A: It's worth analysing from that angle. Sometimes the owner's vision has grown but the identity still represents the older business direction. In scientific logo design we look at whether the logo is aligned with the sales, customer attraction, communication, and growth objective. You can share your logo - Maulik can review it personally and share relevant observations.

Q: Payments keep getting stuck. Is my logo connected?
A: If payment issues recur, it's relevant to analyse the identity from the perspective of money flow, stability, customer relationship, and business structure. The goal is to identify visual and energetic factors that may not be supporting the desired payment flow.

Q: Clients delay payments. How does scientific logo design help?
A: Payment delays aren't only a collection issue. The visual identity can be examined from the perspective of payment flow, stability, and customer relationship. To do that properly, we need to see your business category and existing logo.

Q: My deals break at the last moment. Any connection to the logo?
A: If this happens repeatedly, the pattern can be examined from the identity's perspective - communication, customer relationship, stability, direction, and conversion signals. Maulik can share his personal observations in a 15-minute Zoom.

Q: Customers say yes and then cancel. Can this help?
A: A recurring pattern like that is worth analysing. The logo communicates subconsciously; we check whether the visual identity is properly supporting trust, communication, customer connection, and business stability.

Q: We get new customers but few repeat customers.
A: Repeat business is important. Existing customers, new customers, and communication are all considered in the framework. If repeat business is weak, the existing logo can be examined from that angle.

Q: We can't get new customers.
A: A logo doesn't control customers directly, but visual identity shapes perception and communication. Colour, symbolism, visual attraction, and new-customer orientation are all considered against the business's growth objective.

Q: I can't find competent staff.

A: This can be assessed. Identity, from this framework, also includes employees and internal relationships. If retention is a recurring issue, the logo's structure can be examined from the angles of stability, relationships, and organisational needs.

Q: My staff can't take the business to the next level.
A: The objective of scientific logo design is to align the identity with the growth direction you want. If you want to take the business further, the identity should support that vision. Hiring, training, leadership, and management systems are equally important, though.

Q: My business is profitable but growth has plateaued. Should I redesign?
A: Possibly. Profitable and growing aren't the same. If growth has plateaued, evaluating the current identity is useful. We check whether the existing logo only represents the current business or also supports future growth.

# Framework fundamentals

Q: How does this influence the subconscious mind?
A: Visual symbols are perceived at a subconscious level, not just consciously. The owner, employees, vendors, and customers see the logo repeatedly. The objective is to use shape, colour, symbolism, and structure to reinforce the desired message at that level.

Q: Why call the logo a "Yantra"?
A: A Yantra is a purposeful symbolic structure. The logo is treated as the business's visual Yantra - shapes, colours, directions, and symbolism aligned to the business's purpose.

Q: Why is shape so important?
A: Shapes carry different characteristics. Squares are associated with stability, upward triangles with movement and action, circles with infinity and space. Shape selection is chosen based on the business's objective.

Q: What is the connection between colour and business growth?
A: Colour is part of the logo's visual and energetic language. Different colours carry different associations. Colour is not picked by favourite alone - business category, birth-chart considerations, shape, and overall visual balance are all considered.

Q: Can I use my favourite colour?
A: You can share your preference. If a colour isn't suitable to the business objective or analysis, we may suggest alternatives. The goal is a colour that fits the business more than a colour you personally like.
