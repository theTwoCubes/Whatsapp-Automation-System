# Process

Q: What is your logo design process?
A: We start with a 40-question briefing about your business, do mind mapping and hand sketching to explore ideas, develop the three strongest into digital concepts, add colour, black-and-white, and line versions, work on typography and identity, and present everything with mockups.

Q: What's in the 40-question briefing?
A: Business information, design and style preferences, colour choices, likes and dislikes, and other questions that help us understand the business and the kind of identity you're after.

Q: Do I need to know exactly what I want?
A: No. Give honest information about your business and preferences in the briefing - that becomes the foundation for the creative work.

Q: What is mind mapping?
A: A web of words and ideas connected to the business, used to find concepts that can become meaningful visual forms - beyond the obvious industry symbols.

Q: Do you sketch by hand?
A: Yes. Sketching happens before anything is drawn on a computer.

Q: Do you use templates, Canva, or AI-generated logos?
A: No. Every concept comes from the briefing, mind mapping, and original sketching.

Q: Why only three concepts, not thirty?
A: Quality over quantity. We explore many ideas internally, then develop the three strongest properly for presentation.

# Deliverables and versions

Q: Do you provide colour and black-and-white versions?
A: Yes, plus a line-drawing version that shows the underlying visual form.

Q: Why a black-and-white version?
A: A strong logo should stay recognisable without colour. It's how we test the fundamental strength of the design.

Q: Does the logo work at small and large sizes?
A: Yes. The design accounts for both, including simplified treatments for smaller uses.

Q: Do I get the source files?
A: Yes - source-file delivery is confirmed as part of the agreed scope before we finalise.

Q: Which file formats do you provide?
A: The exact formats are confirmed at the start based on where you'll be using the logo - print, web, signage, and so on.

Q: Can I use the logo for print, website, and social media?
A: Yes. The identity is designed for real business use across print and digital.

# Identity, not just a mark

Q: Is this just a logo or a full identity?
A: Full identity - logo, typography, colour treatment, and visual system, presented through mockups.

Q: Why is typography a separate step?
A: The typeface, letterforms, and the spacing between the symbol and the brand name change how the whole identity reads. It's developed after the core direction is set.

Q: Why do you show mockups?
A: A logo needs to be judged in real applications, not as an isolated graphic. Mockups show how the identity behaves in practice.

# Style and originality

Q: Are your logos minimalist?
A: Our approach is fresh, clean, and minimal, but always with a meaningful conceptual connection to the business.

Q: Do you follow design trends?
A: We focus on identities that stay relevant long term rather than chasing trends.

Q: Can you make my logo look exactly like a reference I share?
A: We'll understand what you like about the reference, but we develop an original direction rather than copying another brand's work.

Q: Can you guarantee my logo is unique worldwide?
A: We create original design work, but a formal trademark and legal search is the only way to guarantee worldwide uniqueness.

Q: Can you guarantee trademark approval?
A: We design with trademark considerations in mind, but registration and legal approval can't be guaranteed through the design service.

# References, colours, direction

Q: Can I share reference logos I like?
A: Yes, please include them in the briefing - they help us understand your visual expectations. The final concepts are developed for your business.

Q: Can I tell you which colours I like?
A: Yes, colour preferences are part of the briefing and are considered during identity development.

Q: What if I don't know which colours or style suit my business?
A: That's completely fine. Share your preferences and expectations, and we work from the business context to develop an appropriate direction.

Q: Can you combine multiple ideas into one logo?
A: Yes, where combining directions genuinely makes the concept stronger rather than busier.

Q: What if I don't want an obvious industry symbol?
A: Good - our mind mapping is built to explore less obvious concepts connected to your business, so we don't fall back on generic industry symbols.
